How to use it

Getting it ready

  1. Print the board and the cards, on thick paper if you can.
  2. Laminate them, then cut out the cards.
  3. Stick a Velcro dot on each circle of the board and on the back of each card.
  4. Choose 4 tasks and their rewards with your child, then put up the cards.
  5. Each time they do it, tick a circle with a dry-erase marker. 5 ticks, and they get the reward!
